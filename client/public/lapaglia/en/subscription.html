1. What you are buying

One product: LaPaglia monthly subscription. It unlocks: a second and further pets (with their profiles, health records and reminders), the document and photo archive, the life album and creating new QR tags.

Always free (even without a subscription): one pet's profile, health records and reminders; the nearby services list; the share card; posting and viewing lost and found listings; posting adoption listings and adoption chats; viewing, correcting, deleting and exporting your existing records; importing a backup; deleting your account.

2. Free trial

First-time subscribers get a 7-day free trial (subject to the store's eligibility rules; once per account). The store decides eligibility; the exact terms are shown on the store's payment screen. If you cancel before the trial ends, you are not charged.

3. Price and automatic renewal

When the trial ends, the subscription is charged monthly and renews automatically every month until you cancel. The price is the amount shown by the store on the payment screen (in your local currency; taxes may be included). On the App Store, renewal happens unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the period.

4. Cancellation

Cancellation is done in the store settings; it cannot be done inside the app:

  • iPhone: Settings > your name > Subscriptions > LaPaglia.
  • Android: Google Play > Profile > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions > LaPaglia.

In the app, Settings > My subscription > "Manage in the store" takes you to this page. After cancelling, the subscription continues until the end of the period. Deleting your account does not cancel the subscription; you must also cancel it in the store.

5. Refunds

Refunds follow the store's policy (Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com; Google Play: order history). If the store issues a refund, your subscription entitlement ends.

6. Price changes

If the price changes, the store informs you in advance; where the store's rules require it, your consent is asked. If you do not agree, the subscription ends at the end of the period.

7. Your data when the subscription ends

Your data is never held hostage. If the subscription ends, your records are not deleted or locked: you can view, correct, delete and export them. Reminders you have set are not switched off, and the QR tag you created keeps working. If you have one pet, you can keep adding records and reminders for it for free. If you have more than one pet, you need to subscribe again to add a new pet, record or reminder, and for new documents, album photos and new QR tags.

8. Restoring and changing devices

The subscription is tied to your LaPaglia account. On a new phone, sign in with the same account and use Settings > My subscription > "Restore purchase". One purchase can be linked to only one LaPaglia account.

9. Consumer rights

Your statutory rights as a consumer are not affected. For complaints: nechhlab.global@gmail.com.
