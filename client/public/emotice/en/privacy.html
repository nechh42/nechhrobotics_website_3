Privacy Policy

SCOPE: This policy applies to the Emotice mobile app and is read together with the Personal Data Notice.

DATA COLLECTED: There is no account. Your profile, entries, shelf, analysis results and photos are stored on your phone. Our server keeps only a random device identifier with the number and time of analyses, for the quota. In addition, unless you turn them off, only daily totals of anonymous usage counters are kept (see USAGE COUNTERS).

THIRD PARTIES: (1) Cloudflare (server hosting) and Anthropic (AI): only when you request a photo analysis and give permission. (2) Apple App Store and Google Play: purchases. (3) Open Beauty Facts (open product database): only when you scan a product, and only the barcode number, through our server. (4) MET Norway (Norwegian Meteorological Institute, weather forecast): only if you chose a city for weather, and only that city’s coordinates, through our server. No ads, no advertising identifier and no third-party analytics are used.

DATA SHARED WITH AN AI SERVICE: (1) What is sent: a resized copy (1024 pixels) of the face photo you choose, the app language and a random device identifier created on your phone. Your name, email, location and profile answers are not sent. (2) How it is collected: only when you start a photo analysis, take or choose the photo yourself, and tap "I allow it" after reading the explanation on the analysis screen; if you do not allow it, nothing is sent. (3) Who it is sent to: through our server (Cloudflare, Inc., hosting) to our AI provider Anthropic PBC (Claude model, USA). (4) All uses: only to produce observations about how your skin looks and to count your analyses. It is not used to identify or verify you, for advertising or profiling, it is not sold, and it is not used to train AI. (5) Protection: Cloudflare and Anthropic process this data only on our behalf, to provide the service, under their commercial terms of service and data processing agreements; these provide the same or equal protection as this policy (encrypted transfer, use limited to the purpose, no model training, limited retention). Anthropic may keep the data for up to 30 days to check for abuse; our server does not store the photo or the result. (6) You can withdraw your permission at any time in Settings > Your permissions.

PRODUCT SCANNING: (1) The photo of a barcode or ingredient list is read ON YOUR PHONE (Apple Vision on iOS, Google ML Kit on Android, on-device); the photo is not sent anywhere and is not kept by the app. (2) If a barcode is read, only the barcode number is looked up in the open product database Open Beauty Facts through our server (Cloudflare) to find the product name and ingredient list. Your device ID, profile and cabinet are not sent; your IP address is not passed to Open Beauty Facts. Our server does not store the search; the answer for a barcode is cached anonymously for up to 7 days. To prevent abuse, an irreversible hash of your IP address and a daily request count are kept (the raw IP is not written). (3) Comparing the ingredients with your cabinet happens on your phone. (4) A scanned product is not added to your cabinet unless you save it.

WEATHER ADVICE: (1) It is optional and turns on only if you choose a city in Settings > City for weather. No location permission is asked and your location is not read; the city list is inside the app. (2) The chosen city is stored on your phone. When the weather is requested, only the city’s centre coordinates are sent to MET Norway through our server (Cloudflare); your device ID, profile, shelf and IP address are not passed to MET Norway. (3) Our server does not store the request; the forecast for the same point is cached anonymously for up to 3 hours. To prevent abuse, an irreversible hash of your IP address and a daily request count are kept (the raw IP is not written). (4) Matching the advice with your shelf happens on your phone. The last forecast is kept on your phone for up to 24 hours. (5) You can remove the city at any time; after that, no weather is requested.

USAGE COUNTERS: (1) To understand at which step people stop using the app, only the fact that “a step was reached” is sent to our server (Cloudflare), at most once per step per installation: first opening, finishing the first questions, the first analysis or first completed routine, opening the Plus screen (with the section it was opened from), tapping the purchase button, the store confirming the purchase, and opening the app again the next day and in the second week. Only the app version, platform (Android/iOS), app language and whether the app was installed with this version are sent with it. (2) No device identifier, profile, photo, answers, shelf, location or city is sent. The server does not store individual events; it only adds one to that day’s total. These totals cannot be linked to a person. To prevent abuse, an irreversible hash of the IP address is counted for one day; the raw IP is not written. (3) No third-party analytics or advertising service is used. (4) You can turn this off at any time in Settings > Anonymous usage counters; while it is off, nothing is sent.

NO SELLING, NO TRAINING: Your personal data is not sold, rented or shared for advertising or marketing; your photos are not used to train AI.

COOKIES AND TRACKING: There are no cookies and no tracking across apps or websites.

NOTIFICATIONS: Turned on with your permission and scheduled on your phone; nothing is sent to a server. No sales notifications are sent. You can turn them off in Settings > Reminders.

BACKUP: The encrypted backup file is created on your phone with a password you choose and saved where you choose. The password is not stored anywhere; we cannot open the file.

SECURITY: Data is kept in the app’s private storage; communication with the server is encrypted with HTTPS; the server does not store photos or results and does not log content. We recommend using a screen lock on your phone.

CHILDREN: Emotice is for people aged 18 and over; we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

CHANGES: If this policy changes, the version and date are updated; for important changes the app asks for your consent again.

CONTACT: nechhlab.global@gmail.com

Personal Data Notice (KVKK)

This notice is prepared under Article 10 of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK).

DATA CONTROLLER: Nechh Robotics. Requests and contact: nechhlab.global@gmail.com

BASIC PRINCIPLE: There is no account. Your data is stored on your phone; it is not saved on our servers and we have no access to it.

DATA PROCESSED: (1) Profile answers: age range, gender, skin type, sensitivity, main concerns, shaving, makeup, routine time and optional “Getting to know you” answers: skincare experience, lifestyle (time outdoors, exercise, sleep), skin tone and monthly skincare budget (on the phone; skin tone is optional, “I’d rather not say” can be chosen, and it is never sent to any server). (2) Daily entries: routine ticks and check-ins (skin feeling and tags) (on the phone). (3) Shelf: products you add (on the phone). (4) Photos and analysis results (on the phone). (5) When you request a photo analysis: a resized photo of your face and your chosen language (for that analysis only, not stored). (6) Quota information: a random device identifier generated on your phone, the number of analyses and the time of the last analysis (on our server; this record contains no name, email or photo). (7) Purchases: payment is handled by Apple or Google; card details never reach us. (8) City for weather: optional; stored on your phone. When the weather is requested, only the city’s centre coordinates are sent; they are not linked to your identity.

FACE PHOTO: Your face photo is processed only to assess how your skin looks; it is not used to recognise you or verify your identity. Whether the photo is suitable for analysis (face, light, angle, sharpness) is checked on your phone; the photo is not sent anywhere for this check. Sending it for analysis depends on your separate, explicit consent.

TECHNICAL CONNECTION DATA: Our server’s hosting provider (Cloudflare) may briefly process technical connection records such as IP address and request time for security and abuse prevention; these records contain no photo or result.

ANONYMOUS USAGE COUNTERS: To improve the app, unless you turn them off, the fact that a step in the app was reached (for example, finishing the first questions or opening the Plus screen) is sent to our server (Cloudflare) together with the app version, platform and language, and is only added to daily totals. No device identifier or any information identifying you is sent or stored; the totals cannot be linked to a person. You can turn this off in Settings > Anonymous usage counters.

PURPOSES: providing your care plan and tracking, photo analysis, applying the analysis quota, providing a purchased subscription, improving the app through anonymous daily total counters, and legal obligations.

LEGAL GROUNDS: establishing and performing the contract (Art. 5/2-c), legitimate interest (Art. 5/2-f) and, where required, explicit consent (Art. 5/1, Art. 9; Art. 6 where data may count as special category).

TRANSFER ABROAD: For a photo analysis, your photo is sent over an encrypted connection via our server (Cloudflare) to our AI provider (Anthropic, USA). This happens only with your explicit consent; without it, every feature except analysis works. The photo and result are not stored on our server. If you turned on weather advice, only the centre coordinates of your chosen city are sent to MET Norway (Norway) through our server; this does not identify you and is not linked to your identity.

NO AI TRAINING: Emotice does not use your photo to train any model. Under its commercial API terms, Anthropic does not use this content for model training by default and deletes it within 30 days, except to review usage-policy violations or where required by law.

RETENTION: Data on your phone stays until you delete it or remove the app (Settings > Delete all my data). The quota record on our server consists only of the device identifier and counts. Usage counters consist only of daily totals and cannot be linked to a person.

YOUR RIGHTS (Art. 11): to learn whether your data is processed, request information, learn the purpose, know the third parties it is transferred to, and request correction, deletion, objection and compensation. Requests: nechhlab.global@gmail.com

RESPONSE TIME: Requests are answered free of charge within 30 days at the latest (Art. 13); if you find the answer insufficient, you may complain to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority.

You can withdraw your explicit consent for photo analysis at any time in Settings > Your permissions.
